Precision in every detail

Details make the difference.

A good cut is decided in the details: the line of the fade, the shape of the beard, the last pass of the razor.

  1. Cut

    Length and fade set to the shape of your head and the way your hair grows.

  2. Shape

    Beard and outline shaped to suit your face, kept in proportion with the cut.

  3. Finish

    Clean edges and styling, so the look holds after you leave the chair.
